
Prevents acute [[COVID]] related injury, and treats long-COVID related brain fog and other neurological symptoms, with possible greater efficacy from benfotiamine.

Benfotiamine, a fat soluble form of thiamine, has greater affinity for nervous system tissue, and maintains longer effect than thiamine's loss in hours as a water soluble vitamin.

High dosage slows proliferation of many forms of cancer, and causes many forms of cancer to undergo apoptosis. Low dosage may accelerate cancer growth.
