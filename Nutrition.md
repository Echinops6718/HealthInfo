
Consuming plenty of choline and phospholipid rich foods, a few grams of betaine (trimethylglycine/TMG), a couple of grams of creatine, and a good b vitamin supplement per day supports the majority of the work of the methylation cycle, allowing the body to more easily maintain and repair tissues. This is especially important when under sustained increased rates of tissue damage, such as chronic illness. [[Nicotinic Acid (niacin)]] supplementation can also help to clear away excess homocysteine from methylation if it's not being recycled back to methionine efficiently.
