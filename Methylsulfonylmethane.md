Reduces joint, muscle, and bladder pain and inflammation, as well as allergic rhinitis.

Reduces arthritis symptoms and general cytokine expression, and improves cartilage healing and preservation from degradation.

Reduces risk of light induced retinal degeneration, though may be contraindicated with glaucoma.

Improves and modulates immune response, and likely improves wound healing.

Crosses the blood brain barrier, and likely reduces brain inflammation.

Likely improves methylation cycle functionality.

Likely reduces pulmonary hypertension, protects against kidney injury and liver toxicity, and assists in rebuilding digestive tract lining.

Likely improves skin smoothness and softness through increasing keratin, and strengthens nails.

Likely protects against BPA and radiation induced neurodegeneration.

Likely protects against several forms of cancer, and improves effectiveness of other medicine with cancers.

Likely increases sensitivity to alcohol.

Likely reduces exercise induced oxidative stress.

May treat eczema, rosacea, and psoriasis.

May reduce the risk of insulin resistance and type 2 diabetes.

Generally well tolerated by most people up to 4 grams per day, though some people take higher dosages long term without issue. There are anecdotal recommendations to start with a low dose, such as 100mg, and gradually increase dosage over the course of weeks to help the body adapt to MSM effects.
