

Reduces risk of Parkinson's and improves Parkinson's symptoms. 

Treats ADD/ADHD.

It downregulates serotonin in the body, though simultaneously supplementing l-tryptophan can reduce that effect

DLP can cause/increase bipolar or mania in people who are susceptible, and is contraindicated for anyone taking antipsychotic medication.

DLP potentiates both stimulants and depressants, so can present risks to intake within days of supplementing.

One dose of DLP anecdotally affects the body for multiple days, and many people consider the experience of supplementing it similar to Adderall.

Can build a tolerance within a few months and lose all positive benefits. Cycling supplementation may reduce loss of effects.
