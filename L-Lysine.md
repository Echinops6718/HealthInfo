
Consuming 2 to 3 grams per day can likely prevent acute [[COVID]] symptoms and reduce long-[[COVID]] symptoms as well. Do not exceed 3 grams per day to prevent bradykinin buildup and potential neurological symptoms.

May prevent symptoms of herpes viruses.

A diet high in L-arginine may counter antiviral benefits of L-lysine.

Contraindicated with cardiovascular or gallbladder disease.
